# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else
the project lives, is expected to be respectful and constructive.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Use plain, welcoming language, and help newcomers find their way.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the contact route in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may block anyone who
breaks this code. Reports are handled confidentially.
